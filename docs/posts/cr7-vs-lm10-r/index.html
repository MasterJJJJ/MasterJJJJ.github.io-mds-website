Question and scope

Do the players have different scoring profiles in this historical club-goal dataset? I use R to count the recorded goals and compare the proportion scored at home and away. Each row is one goal, so these are not counts of matches or scoring rates per appearance.

Load and inspect the data

Summarize goals and date coverage

I split the data by player to check whether the time spans are comparable before interpreting totals.

by_player <- split(stats, stats$Player)
summary_table <- do.call(rbind, lapply(names(by_player), function(player) {
  goals <- by_player[[player]]
  data.frame(
    Player = player,
    Goals = nrow(goals),
    First_date = min(goals$Date),
    Last_date = max(goals$Date)
  )
}))
kable(summary_table, row.names = FALSE)
Player Goals First_date Last_date
Cristiano Ronaldo 710 2002-10-07 2023-03-18
Lionel Messi 703 2005-05-01 2023-03-04

The file contains 710 goals for Ronaldo and 703 for Messi. Their records begin in different years and end in March 2023. These totals describe this snapshot, not their full careers today.

Compare home and away goals

I inspect the venue codes before calculating percentages. Each player’s denominator is their total number of recorded goals, not their number of home or away matches.

stopifnot(!anyNA(stats$Venue), all(stats$Venue %in% c("H", "A")))
stats$Venue_label <- factor(stats$Venue, levels = c("H", "A"),
                            labels = c("Home", "Away"))
venue_counts <- table(stats$Player, stats$Venue_label)
venue_percent <- prop.table(venue_counts, margin = 1) * 100
kable(venue_counts, caption = "Recorded goals by player and venue.")

Visualize the venue comparison

barplot(venue_percent, beside = TRUE,

        ylim = c(0, 100), ylab = "Share of recorded goals (%)",
        xlab = "Venue", border = NA)
legend("topright", legend = rownames(venue_percent),
       fill = c("#2166ac", "#b35806"), bty = "n")

What I found

In this snapshot, Ronaldo scored 404 goals at home and 306 away. Messi scored 419 at home and 284 away. The home shares are 56.9% and 59.6%, respectively.

This comparison shows where the recorded goals occurred. It cannot establish a home advantage or compare player ability: we would need match appearances, minutes played, and opponent information for both scoring and non-scoring matches. Different career coverage and competitions also limit the comparison.
